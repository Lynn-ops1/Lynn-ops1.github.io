About

Hello

Hello and welcome to my homepage! I am a second-year PhD student in Urban Development and Management at the School of Public Affairs, Zhejiang University, where I am very fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Xuewen Li. I hold a B.A. (Honors) in Economics from Minzu University of China, where I graduated as Beijing Outstanding Graduate and was named one of the Top 10 Outstanding Students university-wide. You can find my CV here.

My research lies at the intersection of urban economics, land markets, and industrial policy. Feel free to reach out — I am always happy to chat!
