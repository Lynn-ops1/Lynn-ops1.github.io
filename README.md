# Hi, I'm Liurulin Ji

I am a Ph.D. student in Urban Development and Management at the School of Public Affairs, Zhejiang University.

My research lies at the intersection of urban economics, land markets, industrial policy, local public finance, and the political economy of development. I am particularly interested in how local governments use land as a fiscal and policy instrument, how industrial land subsidies shape firm behavior and local development, and how urban institutions affect spatial inequality in China.

## Research Interests

* Urban Economics
* Land Markets
* Industrial Policy
* Local Public Finance
* Political Economy of Development
* Chinese Economy

## Current Research

My current work studies industrial land allocation and local government fiscal incentives in China. I use parcel-level land transaction records, firm registration data, tax survey microdata, and spatial matching methods to examine how governments subsidize anchor firms and recapture fiscal value through surrounding land markets.
